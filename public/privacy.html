Privacy Policy

Arrow Exit 3D: Escape Puzzle · last updated 14 October 2026

Arrow Exit 3D ("the game", "we") is a puzzle game for Android and iOS published by Jayesh Savaliya. This page says what the game does with data. The short version: the game has no accounts, asks for nothing about you, keeps your progress on your phone, and shows ads through Google AdMob, which uses your device's advertising identifier unless you say no.

What stays on your device

Your level progress, stars, coins, chosen skins, sound and look settings and the day you opened the daily chest are stored only on your phone. We do not have a server that receives them. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

Ads

The game shows ads (app-open, interstitial, native and rewarded video) served by Google AdMob. To do that, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share with Google and its advertising partners:

  • your device's advertising identifier (Android advertising ID or iOS IDFA, where you allow tracking),
  • device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version, IP address and the approximate location it implies),
  • ad interaction events (which ads were shown, viewed or tapped) and diagnostic data.

Google's use of this data is described in the Google privacy & terms and the AdMob data practices.

Your choices

  • Consent form: where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area and the UK), the game shows a consent form on first launch. You can change your answer any time in Settings → Privacy options.
  • iOS tracking prompt: on iPhone the system asks whether the game may track you across apps. Saying no still lets the game show ads, just less tailored ones.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads; iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking).

Remote settings

The game fetches a small configuration file from Firebase Remote Config (Google) to switch ads and tune the game without an update. Firebase may record standard request data such as an app instance identifier and basic device information. We do not use Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics or any sign-in.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.
